Built on modern technology and an offline AI model, «Nama» gives users a simple, fast and reliable way to recognize banknotes and their denominations.

Nama is a smart app that recognizes the denominations of the country's banknotes in circulation. It was designed to make identifying every banknote accurate and easy, with fast access to information even offline. Users scan a banknote, or pick a photo from the app's gallery, and see its details and features.
